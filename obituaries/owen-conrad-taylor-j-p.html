In loving memory

Owen
Conrad Taylor J.P.

Aged 75 · Entered rest April 3rd, 2026

In memory

Affectionately known as “John or Mubutu”.

Of Arch Hall, St. Thomas.

Retired Inspector of the Barbados Police Service, former Justice of the Peace, former choir member of the St. Joseph and St. Thomas Parish Churches, former Sexton, Men’s Fellowship and honorary member of the Mother’s Union of the St. Thomas Parish church.

Family

  • Son of the late Wilfred Cheltenham and Eugene Taylor.
  • Beloved husband of Juliet Taylor.
  • Loving father of Andrew Taylor (Barbados Fire Service), Julia Edwards (Salvation Army) and the late Anne Taylor.
  • Proud grandfather of Michael Taylor.
  • Dear brother of Ermine Watkins(nee Taylor), Emmerson and the late Earl, Eunitha (Ella), Newland and Muriel Taylor and Inez Gooding.
  • Cherished uncle of Roy, Ted, Rodney, Richard, David, Basil, Andwele, Dorian (Junior), Darrien, Georgia, Robert, Elton, Suzette and many others.
  • Great uncle of many.
  • Cousin of Melbourne, Elon, Monica, Pshawnetta, Jeddar, Milton, Justice Carlise Greaves, and many others.
  • Godfather of many.
  • Father-in-Law of Camille Blades-Taylor.
  • Son in Law of the late Seymor Allman and Alma Graham (nee Farmer).
  • Brother-in-law of Robert, Carl, Grace, Marlese, Sandra, Lola, Judith, Mariette, Denise, Herbert, Marvo and the late Denise.
  • Friend of past & present members of the Barbados Police Service, Legal Fraternity of Barbados, Lowe, Rock, Farmer & Allman families, St. Thomas & St. Joseph church families, Modern Old Scholars, Coffee Gully & Branchbury communities of St. Joseph & Arch Hall, St. Thomas & many others too numerous to mention.
Sunrise & SunsetJanuary 1st, 1951 – April 3rd, 2026
